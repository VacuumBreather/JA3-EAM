# 🛡️ Enemy Avoid Militia

> **Stop being a speed bump. Become a wall.**

Are you tired of Diamond Shipments driving through your elite mercenary squads like they're on a Sunday stroll? Do Legion attack squads treat your heavily fortified militia outposts as a minor traffic inconvenience?

**Enemy Avoid Militia** re-educates the AI to respect your personal space. By overhauling the satellite pathfinding logic, this mod forces enemy squads to perceive your presence — mercs and militia alike — as a massive strategic deterrent.

---

## ✅ Features

### 🎯 Tactical Deterrence
Enemies now calculate routes *around* sectors where your mercenaries or militia are stationed, unless that sector is their intended target. No more casual strolls through your lines.

### 💎 Smart Diamond Shipments
Dynamic diamond shipments will no longer commit suicide by walking into your militia. They will actively seek the path of least resistance — forcing *you* to stay mobile and cut them off yourself.

### ⚡ Optimized Performance
Features a custom-coded, high-speed pathfinding engine to ensure diamond shipments always update their routes according to the current strategic situation, without slowing down the game.

### 🤝 Player-Friendly
These changes **only** affect enemy factions (The Legion and Diamond Shipments). Your own mercs have completely unchanged pathfinding.

## ⚠️ Compatibility

> If you use other mods that modify satellite pathfinding logic, you might want to check with the other mod author regarding compatibility.
